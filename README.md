# 🧑‍💼 **HR Analytics Dashboard**

**Power BI — Employee Attrition | Workforce Insights | KPI Reporting**

This project provides a complete **HR analytics dashboard** built in **Power BI**, focusing on employee attrition, workforce distribution, job satisfaction, and demographic analysis.  
It enables HR teams to understand workforce trends, identify high-risk segments, and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🚀 **Project Objectives**

This dashboard aims to analyze:

- Workforce demographics  
- Employee attrition & retention  
- Job satisfaction levels  
- Education field distribution  
- Age group trends  
- Department-wise attrition  
- Gender segmentation  
- KPIs for overall workforce health  

---

## 📈 **Key Insights**

### **1. KPI Summary**
- **Overall Employees:** 1470  
- **Active Employees:** 1233  
- **Attrition:** 237  
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.12%  
- **Average Age:** 36.92  

### **2. Department-Wise Attrition**
- Highest attrition in **R&D (56.12%)**  
- Sales accounts for **38.82%** of attrition  
- HR shows lowest attrition  

### **3. Age Group Workforce Distribution**
- Highest employee count in **25–34** age group  
- Male employees dominate most groups  

### **4. Job Satisfaction Analysis**
- Satisfaction levels across job roles (1–4 scale)  
- "Laboratory Technician" and "Research Scientist" have highest employee counts  

### **5. Education Field Attrition**
- Highest attrition among:  
  - **Life Sciences**  
  - **Medical**  
  - **Marketing**  

### **6. Attrition Rate by Age & Gender**
- Visual donut charts showing high attrition in **younger and mid-career groups**
